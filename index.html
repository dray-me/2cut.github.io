How it works

1

Upload

Drop in any photo. It is processed locally on your device — never uploaded to a server.

2

Slice

Pick 2–6 panels, a direction, and a shape. Square (1:1) and portrait (4:5 / 3:4) are centre-cropped automatically.

3

Post

Download the panels and add them to Instagram in order. Swiping reveals one continuous image.
